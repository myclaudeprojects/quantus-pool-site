How pool shares work

Buying the token only shares you into the pool (proportional hold). It is not required to download or mine. Pre-token balances are stubs when TOKEN_ADDRESS is empty.

1. Mine without buying

Download is open. Run the miner and register a wallet anytime. Token hold is optional and never gates the package.

2. Buy / hold → pool share %

Hold ≥ MIN_HOLD Argus to be eligible. Weight is linear in balance: more tokens → larger share of provisional pool rewards.

3. Connect for a boost

Keep a recent heartbeat (“connected”). Your weight is multiplied by CONNECTED_MULTIPLIER (default 1.5×) on top of holdings.

Formula

weighti = balancei × (connected ? CONNECTED_MULTIPLIER : 1)
sharei = weighti / Σ weights
estQtc = sharei × recent_accrual_qtc  ·  estUsd = estQtc × QTC_USD

Estimates are provisional (demo/stub accrual × live CoinGecko price for id quantus) until claims open. Check your wallet on the dashboard. Price: coingecko.com/en/coins/quantus.

How the pool works

Honest path from GPU to provisional credit — then claim after launch.

Register

Submit your wallet address. You get a miner ID + API key for heartbeats from the one-click package or your own miner.

Claim later

When CLAIMS_OPEN flips true, request payout against your provisional credit. Locked by default today.
