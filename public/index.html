How pool shares work

Your pool % is your slice of mined L1 Quantus in the operator pot — not a fixed % of Arc token supply, and not a fee split. Buying Arc QTC on Argus raises your weight; mining without holding is still open. Arc QTC ≠ L1 Quantus.

1. Mine without buying

Download is open. Run the miner and register a wallet anytime. Token hold is optional and never gates the package.

2. Hold Arc QTC → weight

Hold ≥ MIN_HOLD Arc QTC to be eligible. Weight is linear in balance: more tokens → larger claim on the mined pot.

3. Connect for 1.5× weight

Fresh heartbeat (“connected”) multiplies weight by CONNECTED_MULTIPLIER (default 1.5×). Boosts weight once — does not mint tokens or double-count your balance.

Mined-pot formula

weighti = balancei × (connected ? 1.5 : 1)
pool_sharei = weighti / Σ weights  ← % of mined L1 QTC pot
your_qtc = pool_sharei × pool_mined_qtc

Eligible holders’ shares always sum to 100% of the pot being distributed. Estimates are provisional until claims open. Check the dashboard.

Pool % at a glance

Two different percentages — don’t mix them.

2. Share of mined L1 Quantus

When claims open, each eligible wallet gets:
weight = holdings × (1.5 if connected else 1)
pool_share = weight / Σ weights → % of mined L1 QTC pot

Honest example (hold 10% of Arc supply):
• Everyone ×1 → 10% of the mined pot.
• You connected 1.5×, others ×1 → weight 15 / (15+90) ≈ 14.29% of the pot (not a flat “15%” — denominator grows with your boost).

Launch bag check: house 5M connected vs 95M idle others ≈ 7.3% of the pot (5M is only 0.5% of 1B supply).
